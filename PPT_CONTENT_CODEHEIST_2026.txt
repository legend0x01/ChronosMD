SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM WE HAVE SOLVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[SLIDE TITLE]
The Crisis of the "Missing Context" in Modern Healthcare

[CORE PROBLEM STATEMENT]
A patient's medical life is scattered across hospitals, diagnostic labs, handwritten prescriptions, and radiologic imaging. In a 3-minute emergency consultation, missing historical context kills.

[KEY PAIN POINTS SOLVED]
1. The Fragmentation Trap:
   - Records exist in isolated silos (AIIMS, Apollo, Fortis, local labs). Critical baseline data is lost between transfers.
2. Buried Multi-Year Trends:
   - A single Serum Creatinine of 1.4 mg/dL is meaningless without knowing whether baseline was 0.8 (acute injury) or 2.1 (renal recovery).
3. Invisible Contradictions & Care Gaps:
   - Conflicting drug allergies, duplicate prescription escalations, and unperformed biopsy recommendations fall through clinical cracks.
4. The Fallacy of False Certainty:
   - Modern AI tools flatten nuanced doctor doubts ("provisional", "cannot rule out") into fake settled diagnoses.
