1.1 THE CRISIS OF THE MISSING CONTEXT
In India's fragmented healthcare ecosystem, patients navigate an uncoordinated
maze of tertiary hospitals (AIIMS, Apollo, Fortis, Manipal), neighborhood
clinics, municipal dispensaries, and independent pathology labs (Dr. Lal PathLabs,
Metropolis, SRL). Over a lifetime, a chronic patient accumulates hundreds of
physical papers:
- Faded thermal printouts of ECGs and ultrasound summaries
- Handwritten doctor prescriptions with illegible abbreviations
- Multi-page digital pathology lab reports (PDFs)
- Hospital discharge summaries with disparate diagnostic codings

When a medical crisis occurs, the attending physician has less than 3 to 5 minutes
to understand years of complex, interdependent clinical history. The missing
context leads directly to:
1. Accidental duplicate testing costing families thousands of rupees.
2. Dangerous medication interactions and unrecognized contraindications.
3. Unidentified progressive organ decline (e.g., creeping renal insufficiency).
4. Loss of diagnostic hedging where preliminary hypotheses are wrongly treated as
   settled facts.

1.2 THE CHRONOSMD CORE PHILOSOPHY
ChronosMD is strictly an INFORMATIONAL RECORD-ORGANIZATION PLATFORM.
- ZERO HALLUCINATION: ChronosMD never invents data, predicts unverified diseases,
  or attempts autonomous diagnosis.
- 100% PROVENANCE ATTRIBUTION: Every event, metric, and medication is anchored
  directly to a verified source document excerpt.
- PRESERVATION OF UNCERTAINTY: Medical nuances, physician doubts, and hedging
  language are treated as first-class citizens.

REQUIREMENT 1: IDENTIFY CHANGES ACROSS REPORTS OVER TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 PROBLEM SPECIFICATION
Single medical encounters are static snapshots. Human clinicians cannot easily
mentally reconstruct the longitudinal curve of a patient's health trajectory
when reading disconnected documents months or years apart. The platform must
automatically detect, categorize, and visually display temporal changes across
successive encounters.

1.2 CHRONOSMD IMPLEMENTATION & CORE FEATURES

A. Dynamic Longitudinal Timeline Engine
   - Chronological Event Normalization: Parses disparate date formats (e.g.,
     "14/05/2023", "Oct 12, 2022", "2024-01-08") into standardized ISO-8601
     timestamps, rendering a unified, vertical chronological journey.
   - Elapsed Interval Calculation: Automatically computes the exact duration
     between successive medical encounters (e.g., "+42 days after initial consult",
     "+6 months follow-up"), allowing clinicians to instantly recognize the tempo
     of disease progression.

B. Automated "Interval Change" Highlighting
   - For every sequential encounter, ChronosMD compares key metrics and clinical
     status against the preceding document.
   - Generates an explicit "Interval Longitudinal Change" callout banner on the
     timeline card (e.g., "HbA1c increased from 7.1% to 7.8% (+0.7%)", "Metformin
     escalated from 500mg BD to 1000mg BD", "New symptom: Bilateral pedal edema").

C. Multi-Category Filtering System
   - Instant filtering across three distinct clinical categories:
     1. Clinical Visits / Consultations (Blue badges)
     2. Diagnostic Pathology Labs (Emerald badges)
     3. Pharmacotherapy Prescriptions (Purple badges)
   - Filter chips display live record counts (e.g., Visits: 2, Labs: 2, Prescriptions: 1).

D. 100 Authentic Indian Patient Profiles
   - Pre-loaded with 100 diverse, clinically realistic Indian patient profiles
     spanning 28 Indian states and Union Territories (Maharashtra, Delhi, Tamil
     Nadu, Karnataka, West Bengal, Kerala, Punjab, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana,
     Assam, Rajasthan, etc.).
   - Genuine medical conditions: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, Essential Hypertension,
     Acute Coronary Syndrome, Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 3, Pulmonary Tuberculosis,
     Hashimoto's Thyroiditis, Dengue Fever with Thrombocytopenia, COPD, and more.
   - Contains over 500 structured timeline events, 200 longitudinal lab metrics,
     300 medication histories, and 200 documented discrepancies.

E. Dynamic "Add Report" Timeline Injection
   - Clinicians can inject a new clinical document directly into the patient's
     active timeline using the "+ Add Report" modal.
   - The engine automatically recalculates interval elapsed times, updates category
     counters, and scrolls directly to the newly synthesized event node.

REQUIREMENT 2: TRACK REPEATED TESTS AND MEDICATION HISTORY
================================================================================

2.1 PROBLEM SPECIFICATION
In chronic disease management (Diabetes, Renal Failure, Dyslipidemia, Thyroid
Disorders), an isolated laboratory value is clinically meaningless without its
temporal trajectory. A Serum Creatinine of 1.4 mg/dL represents improvement if the
prior reading was 2.1 mg/dL, but signals acute kidney injury if the baseline was
0.8 mg/dL. Similarly, patients frequently visit different specialists who modify,
substitute, or escalate drugs without a single unified medication ledger.

2.2 CHRONOSMD IMPLEMENTATION & CORE FEATURES

A. BOX 3: Repeated Lab Metrics Trend Tracker
   ChronosMD dedicates an entire focused analytical division (Box 3) to tracking
   recurring biochemical and physical analytes across time:
   - Tracked Biomarkers:
     * Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c %)
     * Fasting & Post-Prandial Blood Glucose (mg/dL)
     * Serum Creatinine (mg/dL) & Estimated GFR (mL/min/1.73m2)
     * Systolic & Diastolic Blood Pressure (mmHg)
     * Serum Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH uIU/mL)
     * Total Cholesterol, LDL-C, Triglycerides (mg/dL)
     * Complete Blood Count: Platelets, Hemoglobin, TLC

B. Algorithmic Delta & Directional Variance Engine
   - Calculates exact numerical variance between consecutive tests:
     Delta = Value(Current) - Value(Previous)
   - Formats human-readable delta indicators:
     * "+0.7% (Elevated / Deteriorating)"
     * "-0.3 mg/dL (Renal Function Improvement)"
     * "+14 mg/dL (Interval Glycemic Spike)"
   - Color-Coded Clinical Status Badging:
     * CRITICAL (Rose theme): Exceeds acute clinical panic thresholds.
     * ELEVATED / HIGH (Amber theme): Outside standard adult reference interval.
     * NORMAL / CONTROLLED (Emerald theme): Within physiologic target ranges.
   - Visual Progress Bars: Normalized horizontal progress bars mapping measured
     values against biological reference bounds.

C. BOX 4: Longitudinal Pharmacotherapy & Prescription History
   ChronosMD aggregates every prescription into a longitudinal medication registry
   (Box 4), organizing prescriptions across multiple healthcare facilities:
   - Structured Medication Attributes:
     * Generic & Brand Drug Name (e.g., "Tab. Metformin 500mg", "Tab. Telmisartan 40mg")
     * Prescribed Strength & Exact Dosage
     * Direction & Timing (e.g., "1 tablet twice daily after meals", "OD empty stomach")
     * Clinical Indication (e.g., "First-line glycemic control", "Nephroprotection")
     * Prescribing Physician & Institutional Facility
     * Start Date & Discontinuation / Escalation Timestamps
   - Lifecycle Status Tracking:
     * ACTIVE (Emerald badge): Currently ongoing pharmacotherapy.
     * DOSE ESCALATED (Blue badge): Dose increased following abnormal lab findings.
     * DISCONTINUED / SWAPPED (Slate badge): Deprecated or replaced due to adverse
       effects or clinical escalation.

D. Dynamic Synchronization via Add Report Engine
   - When a user submits a new lab report in the "+ Add Report" modal with analyte
     details (e.g., "HbA1c: 8.2%"), ChronosMD automatically:
     1. Matches the analyte against existing entries in repeatedMetrics.
     2. Appends the new data point with date and calculated delta variance.
     3. If the analyte is brand-new, dynamically initializes a new longitudinal
        tracker entry.
   - When a user submits a medication (e.g., "Tab. Empagliflozin 10mg OD"),
     ChronosMD registers the drug into the patient's active medication history,
     updating Box 4 live with zero page reloads.

REQUIREMENT 3: CONNECT RELEVANT INFORMATION ACROSS DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

3.1 PROBLEM SPECIFICATION
Medical documents do not exist in isolation. A consultation note on May 1st orders
a blood test; the blood test performed on May 3rd reveals elevated enzymes; this
prompts an ultrasound on May 10th; which culminates in a surgical referral on
May 15th. In standard EHR systems and paper files, these causal linkages are severed,
forcing human reviewers to guess why a test was ordered or why a medication was
abruptly stopped.

3.2 CHRONOSMD IMPLEMENTATION & CORE FEATURES

A. BOX 5: Cross-Document Entity Resolution & Provenance Graph
   ChronosMD models medical records as a Semantic Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG),
   explicitly mapping relationships between discrete clinical encounters:
   - Node: Individual Document / Event (e.g., "IND-PAT-001-EVT-01")
   - Edge: Directed Relationship with Clinical Typology and Provenance Rationale

B. Clinical Relationship Typologies
   ChronosMD recognizes four core relationship classes:
   1. DIAGNOSTIC FOLLOW-UP:
      Links clinical consultation findings or symptoms directly to subsequent
      pathology or imaging orders (e.g., "Consultation on 12 Jan noted bilateral
      pedal edema; prompted Renal Function Panel on 15 Jan").
   2. PHARMACOTHERAPY ESCALATION / ADJUSTMENT:
      Links abnormal diagnostic results to subsequent prescription changes
      (e.g., "HbA1c rising to 7.8% prompted addition of Tab. Empagliflozin 10mg
      in subsequent endocrinology consult").
   3. REFERRAL CONSULTATION:
      Connects general physician encounters to super-specialty interventions
      (e.g., "Internal Medicine consult referred patient to Cardiology following
      borderline Troponin-T levels").
   4. THERAPEUTIC MONITORING:
      Links initiated medication regimens to scheduled safety tests (e.g.,
      "Initiation of Statin therapy connected to 3-month follow-up Liver Function Test").

C. Explanatory Causal Narratives
   - Rather than displaying abstract IDs, Box 5 renders rich, human-readable
     cards detailing the clinical reasoning connecting Document A to Document B.
   - Displays source document tag (`fromEventId`) pointing to destination document
     tag (`toEventId`) with full contextual narrative.

D. Bidirectional Cross-Document Navigation
   - Every relationship card includes interactive citation triggers.
   - Clicking on any linked document citation instantly switches the interface
     to Box 2 (Physical Proof Viewer), displaying the exact physical excerpt
     of the linked document with highlighted text.

E. Dynamic Relationship Creation via Add Report Modal
   - When a new document is added, the user can select an existing prior document
     from a dynamic dropdown and assign a relationship class (Diagnostic Follow-up,
     Medication Escalation, Referral).
   - The engine automatically establishes the bidirectional graph edge, updates
     Box 5 immediately, and stores the relationship persistently.

REQUIREMENT 4: IDENTIFY MISSING OR INCONSISTENT INFORMATION
================================================================================

4.1 PROBLEM SPECIFICATION
Medical errors, diagnostic delays, and adverse drug events are rarely caused by a
lack of data; they are caused by unrecognized inconsistencies across conflicting
documents. For example, a hospital discharge summary states "No Known Allergies"
while an older surgical record lists "Severe Anaphylaxis to Penicillin". Similarly,
recommendations made in doctor notes (e.g., "Repeat USG in 6 months") frequently
fall through the cracks because no system tracks unclosed loops.

4.2 CHRONOSMD IMPLEMENTATION & CORE FEATURES

A. BOX 6: Automated Contradictions & Clinical Uncertainty Engine
   ChronosMD provides a dedicated analytical division (Box 6) that systematically
   audits the longitudinal record for factual discrepancies, care gaps, and
   conflicting clinical statements.

B. Two Distinct Clinical Alert Categories

   1. CONTRADICTION ALERTS (High Severity, Rose Theme, `alert-triangle` icon):
      - Represents direct factual contradictions between two or more documents.
      - Examples detected in ChronosMD patient profiles:
        * Discordant Diagnoses: Outpatient note diagnoses "Mild Stage 1 Hypertension",
          while inpatient summary simultaneously records "Patient normotensive without
          antihypertensives".
        * Incompatible Medication Dosages: Prescription lists Metformin 500mg BD,
          while discharge summary specifies Metformin 1000mg OD.
        * Allergy Discrepancies: Inconsistent drug sensitivity reporting between
          admission records and anesthesiology clearances.
      - Severity Badge: `HIGH RISK` with rose-bordered callout.

   2. MISSING CARE LINKS & OPEN CLINICAL LOOPS (Medium Severity, Amber Theme, `git-branch` icon):
      - Represents recommended clinical actions that have no corresponding
        documentary proof in subsequent timeline events.
      - Examples detected in ChronosMD patient profiles:
        * Unperformed Diagnostic Follow-up: Physician note specifies "Repeat Serum
          Creatinine and Electrolytes in 4 weeks", but no lab report was ingested
          within that temporal window.
        * Missing Baseline Clearance: ACE Inhibitor / ARB prescribed without documented
          baseline serum potassium or renal function assessment.
        * Unresolved Referral: Referral to Ophthalmologist for diabetic retinopathy
          screening documented without subsequent fundoscopy report.
      - Severity Badge: `MEDIUM / ATTENTION` with amber-bordered callout.

C. Direct "Inspect Conflicting Source" Verification Trigger
   - Every contradiction and care gap card features a one-click button:
     "Inspect conflicting source document excerpt ->"
   - Clicking this button immediately navigates to Box 2 (Physical Proof Viewer)
     and loads the exact source document snippet where the discrepancy originated,
     enabling clinicians to resolve contradictions with primary source truth.

D. Dynamic Discrepancy Reporting in Add Report Modal
   - When injecting a new document, the user can check "Flag Clinical Discrepancy /
     Care Gap" to record conflicting observations or missing tests.
   - The engine automatically appends the discrepancy to `patient.discrepancies`,
     updates the Box 6 badge counter, and persists the record.

REQUIREMENT 5: PRESERVE UNCERTAINTY AND SOURCE PROVENANCE
================================================================================

5.1 PROBLEM SPECIFICATION
Modern automated systems and AI summaries frequently suffer from "false certainty"—
they flatten nuanced clinical hedging (*"suspected," "rule out," "borderline"*) into
definitive diagnoses, causing downstream clinicians to perpetuate diagnostic bias.
Furthermore, clinicians will never trust an automated timeline unless every data
point can be visually proven against the original physical hospital document.

5.2 CHRONOSMD IMPLEMENTATION & CORE FEATURES

A. BOX 2: Physical Document Proof Viewer (Split-Screen Provenance)
   ChronosMD incorporates an interactive physical document facsimile viewer (Box 2):
   - Authentic Indian Hospital Letterhead: Displays the originating facility (e.g.,
     "ALL INDIA INSTITUTE OF MEDICAL SCIENCES (AIIMS), NEW DELHI", "APOLLO HOSPITALS,
     GREAMS ROAD, CHENNAI", "TATA MEMORIAL HOSPITAL, MUMBAI").
   - Physical Metadata: Page numbering ("Page 1 of 2"), institutional address,
     and official confidentiality seals ("CERTIFIED CLINICAL UPLOAD", "OFFICIAL
     PATHOLOGY SEAL").
   - Verbatim Extracted OCR Text: Displays raw, unmanipulated text from the physical
     document with highlighted excerpt boxes representing the exact primary evidence.
   - Cryptographic Provenance Hash: Every document displays a simulated SHA-256
     tamper-evident cryptographic verification string (e.g., "SHA-256: 4f8b9...c31e"),
     certifying document integrity and audit trail provenance.

B. First-Class Clinical Hedging & Uncertainty Preservation
   - Rather than eliminating ambiguous phrasing, ChronosMD isolates and highlights
     physician hedging in specialized italicized callouts:
     * '"Borderline microalbuminuria noted; rule out early diabetic glomerulopathy
        versus transient exercise-induced proteinuria."'
     * '"Suspected subclinical hypothyroidism; repeat TSH with anti-TPO in 6 weeks
        before permanent thyroxine commitment."'
     * '"Ground glass opacity noted in right lower lobe; cannot definitively rule
        out atypical viral pneumonitis versus organizing pneumonia."'
   - Preserves diagnostic caution and prevents premature diagnostic anchoring.

C. Standalone Printable Report Page (`report.html`)
   - Every timeline event and proof record can be opened in a dedicated browser tab
     via the "Open in New Page" action (`report.html?patient=...&event=...`).
   - Official Clinical Presentation:
     * High-resolution clinical header with hospital stamp and verification badge.
     * Comprehensive patient demographic grid (Name, Age, Gender, Blood Group, Address).
     * Official Rx Symbol and detailed Pharmacotherapy table.
     * Full Laboratory Analyte Table with reference intervals and status tags.
     * Authentic Doctor Signature in specialized cursive font (`Caveat`) alongside
       official Medical Council Registration (e.g., "Dr. Rajesh Sharma, MD (AIIMS),
       Reg: MCI-2009-0842 / DVA-9941").
   - Production-Grade PDF Download / Print:
     * Clean `@media print` CSS strips navigation buttons and background artifacts.
     * Formats document to standard A4 printable dimensions for official sharing.

D. Add Patient Registration System (Dynamic Profile Expansion)
   - Clinicians can register brand-new Indian patient profiles via "+ Add Patient":
     * Demographic Inputs: Name, Age, Gender, Blood Group, City, State, Full Address.
     * Clinical Profile: Primary Diagnosis, Risk Level, Facility, Attending Doctor.
     * Baseline Clinical Document: Initial title, category, lab analyte, medication,
       hedging phrasing, and raw findings.
   - Generates sequential patient ID (`IN-101`, `IN-102`, etc.), saves to
     `localStorage.chronos_custom_patients`, updates header counters (`101 Patients`),
     and renders immediately across all 6 analytical boxes.

E. Informational Non-Diagnostic Guardrails
   - Prominent UI disclaimer banners across both dashboard and report pages:
     "IMPORTANT NOTICE: ChronosMD is an informational record-organization tool,
     NOT an autonomous diagnostic system. All treatment and clinical decisions
     must be validated by a licensed medical practitioner."
